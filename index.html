About Us

Heart-to-Heart was founded by students at the University of Miami and inspired by a similar program at St. Francis Hospital in New York City. Our mission is to connect cardiac rehabilitation patients with one another, creating a peer support network that helps individuals feel supported, informed, and empowered throughout their health journey.

Heart-to-Heart Connect

Join us for small-group conversations about heart health in a relaxed, welcoming environment. These sessions focus on open discussion, shared experiences, and building meaningful connections.
