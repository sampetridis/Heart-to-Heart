About Us

Heart to Heart is a student-led initiative founded in Spring 2026 by medical students at the University of Miami. Inspired by the work of the BraveHearts program at St. Francis Hospital in New York, Heart to Heart was created to address a critical but often overlooked gap in cardiovascular care: the need for connection, encouragement, and ongoing support during cardiac rehabilitation.

For many patients, recovery after a cardiac event can feel isolating and uncertain. While clinical care provides the foundation for recovery, there is often limited opportunity to connect with others who understand the experience firsthand. Heart to Heart aims to change that by building a supportive community where patients can share their journeys, learn from one another, and feel empowered as they move forward.

Our core mission is to connect cardiac rehabilitation patients with peers who have gone through or are currently navigating similar challenges. By creating a space for open conversation and shared experience, we seek to show patients that recovery is not something they face alone—and that long-term success is both achievable and sustainable.

We organize small-group events that bring patients together in a relaxed, welcoming environment, encouraging meaningful conversation and peer support. In addition, we offer educational sessions focused on lifestyle interventions, recovery strategies, and practical approaches to improving heart health. These initiatives are designed to complement clinical care and provide patients with tools and confidence beyond the hospital setting.

Heart to Heart is focused on serving cardiac rehabilitation patients within the University of Miami community, with plans to expand into Jackson Health System and UHealth networks. Our long-term vision is to scale this model across hospitals throughout the Miami area and eventually extend it nationwide, creating a broader network of patient-centered cardiovascular support.

At its core, Heart to Heart is built on a simple idea: that connection is a powerful part of healing. By bringing patients together, we aim to make the recovery process more supportive, more accessible, and more human.
