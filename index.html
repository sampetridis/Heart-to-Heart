About Us

Heart-to-Heart is a student-led initiative founded in Spring 2026 by medical students at the University of Miami. Inspired by the BraveHearts program at St. Francis Hospital in New York, our mission is to connect cardiac rehabilitation patients and build a peer support network that empowers individuals throughout recovery.
